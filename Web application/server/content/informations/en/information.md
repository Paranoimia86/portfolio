# Course Description

The course focuses on the fundamentals and advanced techniques of scripting and task automation in operating systems. Students will learn to work with tools such as PowerShell and Bash, creating scripts for managing files, processes, and system services. Emphasis is placed on the efficiency, readability, and optimization of scripts, as well as solving practical tasks in the field of system administration. Graduates of the course will be able to independently design and implement scripts for automating various processes.

## Conditions for Obtaining Credit and Grading

Credit for the course is conditional on obtaining a minimum number of points during the semester. The maximum number of points a student can earn is 40 points. The grading consists of several continuous activities that assess both the theoretical knowledge and practical skills of the student:

- Continuous tests (4 tests) – total of 20 points  
  Each test is worth a maximum of 5 points and includes both closed and open-ended questions.
- Semester assignments (3 assignments) – total of 15 points  
  The assignments focus on solving complex practical scripting tasks. The evaluation considers the functionality of the solution, efficiency, code quality, and documentation.
- Activity and work during exercises – 5 points  
  This evaluates the student's ongoing activity, independence in solving tasks, and participation during exercises.  
  To obtain credit, it is necessary to achieve at least 21 points (i.e., 51% of the total score).

## Conditions for Passing the Exam and Grading

The course exam follows the credit acquisition and consists of a written and oral part. The maximum number of points for the exam is 60 points.

- Written part of the exam (test) – maximum of 40 points  
  The written part is conducted in the form of a test containing 40 questions. A time limit of 45 minutes is set for its completion. The test includes both closed and open-ended questions and focuses on verifying theoretical knowledge and practical understanding of scripting and automation. The evaluation considers the correctness of answers and the ability to apply acquired knowledge.
- Oral part of the exam – maximum of 20 points  
  The oral part involves answering questions from a specified range of topics. From the list of questions, 4 are selected, with each question worth a maximum of 5 points. The evaluation considers the level of understanding, factual accuracy of answers, ability to explain, and argumentation.  
  To successfully pass the exam, it is necessary to achieve at least 31 points (i.e., 51% of the total score).

## Attendance Policy

Attendance at practical sessions is mandatory. A student is allowed a maximum of 3 absences during the semester.

If this limit is exceeded, the student is required to arrange a compensation assignment with the instructor, which may take the form of an additional task or an individual project.
